BEFORE THE FIRST FRAME

A few good
questions.

What can I make from screenshots?

This tool is designed for product demos, feature updates, and short launch videos. The motion examples use a fictional app to show how screenshots can tell a product story.

Do I need a paid plan?

Yes. Creating a video requires a paid plan. You can view the prepared examples before choosing one. There is no free generation plan.

Do I need to record my screen?

No recording is needed to start with screenshots. To show an exact live interaction, such as typing or dragging, a screen recording may be a better fit.

Where can I use my video?

Use a product video on your website, in a launch announcement, or in a feature update. Choose a layout that fits where you plan to share it.
