## 1. The service

Screenshots to Video creates short AI-assisted product videos from screenshots. Homepage animations are illustrative previews using a fictional product, not customer work. AI can alter text, interface details and timing; review each result before publishing. We do not guarantee exact pixel preservation, platform approval or marketing performance.

## 2. Paid video credit packs

Current packs cost USD 3.90 for 1 video credit, USD 9.90 for 3, USD 19.90 for 6, and USD 29.90 for 10. These are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. Unused credits have no application expiry. A 5-second video uses 1 credit, 10 seconds uses 2, and 15 seconds uses 3. Credits from multiple current packs can be combined. A new generation consumes credits again; downloading an existing available result does not. Video credits are not SeeAPI credits or the generic AI credits used elsewhere in the account.

Earlier render packs keep their original number of renders, each valid for any supported duration. They are not converted into video credits. Available legacy renders are used before new video credits.

## 3. Reservations and failures

A task reserves the required credits or one legacy render when accepted. A confirmed system failure to produce a video releases that reservation. A completed video whose style, text or interface details you dislike does not include a free redo. A timeout or temporary status-query error does not prove failure: keep the task and check it again. Recovery uses the same request key and provider idempotency key; it does not create a second paid generation. Failed tasks are not automatically restarted as fresh generations.
